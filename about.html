About EcoTipping Points

The EcoTipping Points project started 20 years ago as a website collecting environmental success stories from communities around the world. More than 100 stories document how specific communities reversed ecological decline and what made recovery last.

Systems ecologist Gerry Marten founded the project. His research and teaching span more than 65 years across North America, Latin America, Africa, and Asia. Two of his books provide the conceptual foundation: Traditional Agriculture in Southeast Asia and Human Ecology: Basic concepts for sustainable development. Both are available for free download.

The toolkit distills lessons from those cases for organizations working directly with local communities. It draws on human ecology, the study of relationships between people and their environments, and on decades of fieldwork across the Global South.
