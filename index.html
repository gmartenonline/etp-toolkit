The EcoTipping Points Toolkit

A practical guide for organizations facilitating ecological restoration at community scale.

The toolkit details a structured process for using systems thinking methods with local communities to devise effective and durable solutions for reversing environmental decline. Our approach has been validated by decades of field experience across the rural global South.
