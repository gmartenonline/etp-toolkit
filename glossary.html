Glossary

System
An assemblage of interconnected elements whose behavior is shaped by functional connections between them. The behavior of the entire system is a consequence of interaction between the parts.
Systems thinking
Directing attention to impacts that changing one part of a system will have on the entire system. Systems thinking can help to identify effective action for problem solving.
Social system (= human social system)
Worldview, social organization, social institutions, knowledge, and technology of a community. A community's social system is the basis for how it functions.
Ecosystem
The entire biophysical world in a specified area. The scale of an ecosystem can range from microscopic to the entire Planet. This toolkit focuses on environmental support systems for local communities.
Human-environment system
The combined social system and ecosystem. The way the combined system functions is a consequence of interaction between social system and ecosystem.
EcoTipping Points
Points in a human-environment system where changes small enough to be doable can cross a threshold to set in motion major changes such as decline and collapse or restoration and sustainability.
Negative tipping point lever
Actions or events that set decline in motion in a human-environment system.
Positive tipping point lever
An assemblage of interventions that set in motion the reversal of decline in a human-environment system, putting it on a course of restoration, health, and sustainability. Positive tipping point levers include (1) technology that underlies intervention for desired ecosystem change and (2) social organization to put that technology effectively into use.
Negative tip
Decline in the human-environment system initiated by a negative tipping point lever.
Positive tip
Transformation from decline to restoration of human-environment system triggered by a positive tipping point lever.
EcoTipping Point success story
A "two-act play" that begins with a negative tip and ends with a positive tip. EcoTipping Point success stories offer lessons about what it takes to achieve success. A key feature of the stories is the positive tipping point lever that turns decline around to restoration.
Feedback loop
A continuous circle of cause and effect, where change in one part of the circle induces a chain of effects around the circle to generate further change. The feedback loops in this toolkit are amplifying feedback loops, which can drive dramatic change.
Vicious cycle
A feedback loop that drives undesirable change such as decline in a human-environment system. Vicious cycles can be so powerful that actions intended to solve a problem are ineffective because they are not able to overcome the vicious cycles.
Virtuous cycle
A feedback loop that drives desirable changes such as restoration and sustainability in a human-environment system. Virtuous cycles appear when a positive tipping point lever reverses vicious cycles, transforming them into virtuous cycles. Additional virtuous cycles may appear as community initiatives create a dynamic of "success breeds success" during a positive tip.
Positive spin-offs
New virtuous cycles whose creation is stimulated by existing virtuous cycles, typically a consequence of community initiatives stimulated by virtuous cycles.
Ingredients for Success
Key features of the technology and community organization in positive tipping point levers that increase the effectiveness of a lever. We see the same Ingredients for Success in every success story.
